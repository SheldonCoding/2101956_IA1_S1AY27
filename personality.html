Background

LLMs

LLMs, such as ChatGPT, Claude, and others, are tools to make everyday tasks easier but not a replacement for human creativity and critical thinking. They can assist in generating ideas, providing information, and automating certain tasks, but they lack the ability to truly understand context, emotions, and the nuances of human experience. Therefore, while LLMs can be valuable aids, they should be used in conjunction with human judgment and creativity rather than as a substitute for it.
